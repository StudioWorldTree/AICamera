# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Duke on the brick EVF: watching fractal1’s 3090 while models swap. Not a
public marketing page. `/bay` is a filmmaker tally — tungsten, digits,
what is in the gate.

## Product Purpose

Show what the GPU is doing: VRAM, draw, which cartridges are on the cart
versus in the gate. Success is an honest loadout, not a dashboard of
vanity metrics.

## Brand Personality

Tungsten, tally, waveform. Space Age lock-in, Tactic Extra Extended
Black HUD digits. Vaporwave lives *behind* the VRAM graph as a reaction
layer, not as the chrome of the page.

## Anti-references

Neon-grid as the whole UI. Generic SaaS metric cards. Copying the Tron
Legacy score (copyright). Mag rack that labels every slot ON CART
whether or not anything is resident.

## Design Principles

- The graph is the set; the grid is the sky behind it.
- Mag rack matches the catalog and the process list.
- Spin-up is felt: speed, filter, count — not a toast.
- Reduced motion still reads the numbers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA on type and tally. `prefers-reduced-motion` freezes the grid
and does not start audio. Sound is opt-in (click to arm).
